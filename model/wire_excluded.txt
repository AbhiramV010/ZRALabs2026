# The cropper decides whether a photograph has a band of wire in it at
# all. What it cannot decide is whether that band is the right subject:
# a montage of four catenary photos, a close-up with a white ellipse
# drawn on it, or a street of tram wire over a shopfront all crop
# perfectly and none of them belong in the class. Those are the entries
# below. One filename per line, '#' starts a comment.
